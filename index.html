002 Payload vibration-isolation moduleIREC Rocket Competition Vibration / Dynamics UMass Rocket Team ▾

Overview

As Payload Engineer, I lead development of a MATLAB simulation modeling the response of a vibration-isolation module under launch and parachute-deployment loading. I evaluated 100 candidate spring constants using RMS vibration response metrics, then applied a weighted value-function methodology to select the optimal spring configuration and balance competing stability considerations.

Outcome

  • Selected a spring configuration through a 100-candidate, metric-driven trade study
  • Supported fabrication and assembly of the module using CNC-machined components

Overview

Designed and fabricated a 9'×8' pontoon boat that folds down to fit in a pickup truck bed while retaining structural and buoyancy requirements. Built detailed CAD models in SolidWorks and Onshape, ran FEA to evaluate structural performance under loading, and performed hand calculations for buoyancy, maximum occupancy, and other critical design constraints.

Outcome

  • Carried the design through a full cycle: concept generation → CAD → FEA → fabrication → on-water demonstration
  • Presented a 30-minute design report covering decisions, progress, and outcomes
